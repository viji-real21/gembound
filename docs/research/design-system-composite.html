How a feature-dense app stays simple

  1. Keep one stable shell. The same navigation, content, and contextual-panel regions appear on every app route.
  2. Organize navigation around the user’s main work. It is a mental model, not a full site map.
  3. Show the current task in the center. Secondary detail opens beside it in a split panel or drawer and closes without losing place.
  4. Reveal depth when needed. Advanced controls, help, history, and diagnostics stay contextual instead of competing with the first action.
  5. Adapt by priority. Small screens reflow, hide secondary material, or change the workflow; they do not squeeze every desktop feature into view.
  6. Lock page families and states. Loading, empty, error, permission, success, long copy, keyboard, and reduced motion are designed once and reused everywhere.
