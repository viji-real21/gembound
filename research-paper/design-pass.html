What the pipeline actually did

This is the pass itself, step by step — so you can see where it holds and where it needed correcting.

1 · Reference, read live entries/illustrated-ground-and-mascot.md — inkbox.ai read live, not screenshotted: one hue ramp, one warm accent, one creature at three sizes.
2 · Locked style block One style string in PLATES.json, composed as subject, style for every plate. Per-plate prompt drift is how a series stops looking like one hand.
3 · Local render design-plates.py → mflux / Flux schnell, 768×768. No API, no cost. Each plate must exist, clear a size floor and pass a not-flat check or it re-renders.
4 · Correction in the subject First cut of 2 came back cute and 3 came back a snail. Fixed in the subject line, not with a heavier style block — same lesson as the dreamscape plate.
5 · Gates design-sameness.py for the generic tells, design-shot-critic.py to render it at three sizes with reduced-motion forced.
6 · Your call One word back. Then three refinements of that one, and the app icon comes out of the same mark — which is why the site goes first.
