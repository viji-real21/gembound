# Identity — the Fleet landing page

**Audience.** A solo developer or small-team lead who already pays for one AI coding
seat, is sceptical of "agent" marketing, and reads evidence before pricing. They came
from the research paper or a link that promised numbers, not vibes.

**Outcome they came for.** To decide in under two minutes whether a $319/mo pooled
fleet actually beats the one seat they have — with the coverage math, the failure
modes, and the caveats visible.

**Feeling that earns trust.** *A well-run night watch.* Calm, unhurried competence in
the dark: someone is awake while you sleep, the lamps are on, the logbook is honest.
Never hype, never urgency. The page should feel like standing on a quiet quay at 3am
watching work get done.

**Through-line metaphor.** A working harbour at night. Agents are hulls at berth taking
lanes out; the scheduler is the harbourmaster; the logbook (evidence paper) is a
harbourmaster's ledger; the mascot is a lantern-beacon that stays lit. One light source
(sodium amber) against near-black — the palette IS the metaphor.

**Banned category cues** (what a generic "AI agents" page does that this must not):
- purple/blue gradient meshes, glassmorphism cards, 3D blob renders
- icon-grid "features" rows with stroke icons
- floating screenshots at a jaunty perspective tilt
- "Supercharge your workflow" verb-first hype copy; any unproven number
- rounded-everything sameness; Inter-only typography
- mixed media on one surface (photo + cartoon + vector — the round-2 rejection)

**Honesty rule carried from the skeleton.** Every number keeps its provenance tag
(PROVEN / MEASURED / simulation) — the design may stage the caveat, never bury it.
