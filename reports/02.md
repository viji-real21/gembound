## Forkability

- **No complete internal pipeline is public.** Research intake, approvals, brand governance, human review, DAM handoff, telemetry, and release operations remain undisclosed.
- **Forkable references do exist.** Google’s [Apache-2 Creative Studio](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/gcc-creative-studio) includes frontend, backend, infrastructure, brand-guide input, image/video generation, and CI—but explicitly calls itself an unsupported reference implementation. NVIDIA’s toolkit is Apache-2, while model/plugin licenses vary.
- **Existing standard:** Keep [ComfyUI’s GPL-3 graph/API](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI) rather than building another orchestrator; add official provider SDK/API adapters.

## What this changes for us
Keep ComfyUI as the orchestration core; do not claim major-company internal adoption.
Borrow Google Creative Studio and NVIDIA workflows as forkable references, not “their pipeline.”
Put provider APIs behind adapters and record every model, node, and license per workflow.
